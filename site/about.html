About

I am a rising third-year PhD student in the Department of Biostatistics at Johns Hopkins University, where I am working with Professor Abhi Datta. I am broadly interested in developing and applying statistical methods for extracting insights from complex data in global and environmental health contexts.

I previously worked as a research assistant for Professor Majid Ezzati at Imperial College London, as part of the NCD Risk Factor Collaboration. My work modelling global trends in obesity contributed to co-first-author papers in The Lancet and Nature. I also spent several months as a visiting researcher at AIMS-RiC in Kigali, Rwanda. Before joining ICL I graduated with distinction from the University of Oxford, where I studied mathematics.

Outside of research, I enjoy long bike trips, running, and reading.
