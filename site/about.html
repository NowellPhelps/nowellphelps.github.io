About

Hello! I'm a PhD student in the Department of Biostatistics at Johns Hopkins University, where I am advised by Dr Abhi Datta. I am broadly interested in developing and applying statistical methods to derive insights from complex data in global health and environmental health contexts. My main methodological areas of interest are Bayesian statistics, causal inference, and machine learning.

I also care deeply about statistical education and communication; I am co-lead of JHU Biostatistics Student Organization's Curriculum Committee and am excited to be developing an undergraduate course on data visualization which I will teach in Fall 2027 as a Gordis Teaching Fellow.

Outside of academia, I enjoy cycling, running, and reading. Please feel free to reach out to me at nphelps2 [at] jh [dot] edu!
